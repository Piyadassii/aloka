Per šį atsiskyrimą tyrinėsime, kaip, pasitelkdami meditacijos tyloje ir įžvalgos dialogo praktikos galią, giliai klausydami ir kalbėdami iš tylos, galime būti ir su savimi ir su kitais. Besąlygiškai priimant savo vidinę patirtį ir palaikant santykius su kitais, atsiveria durys atjautai ir išlaisvinančiai įžvalgai.

Per atsiskyrimą vyks ne tik meditacija tyloje, bet ir įžvalgos dialoge meditacija (meditacija santykyje su kitais) grupelėse po du ar daugiau žmonių. Jos metu bus tiksliai nurodoma apmąstymų kryptis, skirta mūsų žmogiškajai patirčiai tyrinėti dabarties akimirkos betarpiškume.


### Apie įžvalgą dialoge

Įžvalga dialoge - tai meditacijos praktika santykyje su kitais (tarpasmeninė meditacija), skirta padėti mums būti budriems kasdienybėje. Ji apjungia meditacinį sąmoningumą, Budos mokymą apie išmintį ir santykių galią, kad padėtų išlaisvinančiai įžvalgai ir atjautai rastis ir augti.

Norėdami sužinoti daugiau apie _Insight Dialogue_, apsilankykite [www.insightdialogue.org](www.insightdialogue.org).

## WAKING UP TOGETHER for all
## Insight Dialogue and Meditation Retreat with Ayya Anopama

**September 11th-13th**

In silence and together, what’s it like to…\
… pause and arrive in the here and now\
… relax and accept things as they are\
… open and expand our awareness in mutuality/to include others\
… attune ourselves to emergence and yield to change, not knowing\
… listen deeply, with the whole body, receive the world and rest in it\
… speak the truth, in kindness.

In this retreat we will explore how we can be present within ourselves and with others through the power of silent meditation and Insight Dialogue practice, listening deeply and speaking from silence. In the unconditional acceptance of our inner experience and in relationship with others, the door to compassion and liberating insight opens.

The retreat will include silent meditation as well as meditation Insight Dialogue (interpersonal meditation) in groups of two or more with a precise direction of contemplation to explore our shared human experience in the immediacy of the present moment.


**About Insight Dialogue**

Insight Dialogue is a relational meditation practice (interpersonal meditation) designed to help us be awake in our everyday lives. It brings together meditative awareness, the wisdom teachings of the Buddha and the power of relationship, to support the development of insight and compassion. 

To find out more about Insight Dialogue visit: [www.insightdialogue.org](www.insightdialogue.org)

**Language**

The teachings will be offered in English with Lithuanian translation.

**Prerequisites**

This retreat is open to all who have prior experience in meditation and/or mindfulness practice. Participants are asked to commit to participating for the entire retreat so we can weave a sense of community together.
The retreat will be held in mindful silence, except for Insight Dialogue practice. Please be prepared not to use your phone or other on-line devices during the retreat to protect your practice and that of others. ​

The teachings and practices offered are grounded in the Buddha’s teachings. Participation does not require adherence to Buddhism or any change in personal faith. 


**Practices of the retreat**

\- Meditation instructions\
\- Sitting, walking and working meditation\
\- Relational meditation in pairs and small groups (Insight Dialogue)\
\- Reflections by the teacher\

**About the teacher**

Ayya Anopama is a Buddhist nun and meditation teacher offering teachings and retreats in English, Spanish, and German across continents. She spent several years in intensive retreat in Myanmar, where she ordained with the Venerable Pa Auk Sayadaw and trained in the Burmese Forest Tradition. For more than two decades, she has practiced with renowned teachers in the Early Buddhist, Tibetan, and Insight Meditation traditions in both East and West. A member of the Global Insight Dialogue Teaching Community and a student of Compassionate Inquiry (CI), she continues to explore how ancient wisdom, contemporary healing, and relational approaches can meet as doorways to inner freedom.
